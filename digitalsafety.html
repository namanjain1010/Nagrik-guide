DIGITAL SAFETY

Stay Safe Online

Simple habits to protect your Account, Money, Personal
information and devices.

Learn Digital Safety.

Protect Your Accounts

Keep your online accounts secure with a few basic habits.

🔒

Use strong passwords

Create unique passwords for important accounts. Avoid using your name, birthday, or phone number.

🛡️

Turn on 2-Step Verification

Add an extra layer of protection to your email, social media, banking, and other important accounts.

🚫

Never share OTPs

Banks and legitimate services will not ask you to share your OTP, PIN, or password.

Spot Online Scams

Scammers often create urgency to make you act without thinking.

🔗

Be careful with unknown links

Don't click suspicious links sent through SMS, WhatsApp, email, or social media.

⚠️

Don't trust urgent payment requests

Someone asking you to immediately send money, scan a QR code, or share an OTP may be trying to scam you.

✓

Check before you pay

Verify the person's identity and payment details before sending money.

Protect Your Personal Information

Your personal information can be valuable to scammers.

👤

Think before you share

Avoid publicly sharing your Aadhaar number, PAN details, passwords, bank information, or other sensitive documents.

🌐

Check website addresses

Before entering personal or payment information, make sure you're using the official website or app.

📱

Keep your phone secure

Use a screen lock and install apps only from trusted sources.

Social Media Safety

Enjoy social media, but stay aware.

⚙️

Review your privacy settings

Choose who can see your posts, profile information, and contact details.

👥

Be careful with strangers

Don't share personal information with people you only know from the internet.

↗️

Think before you post

Information shared online can be copied, saved, or forwarded even after you delete it.

🛡️

Stay Safe

Never share your OTP, PIN, password, or banking details with anyone. When something feels urgent or suspicious, stop and verify it first.

🔔

If You Think You've Been Scammed

Act quickly. Contact your bank or payment service if money has been lost, secure your affected accounts, and report the incident through the appropriate official channel.
